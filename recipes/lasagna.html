Lasagna

Home

Cooking a classic lasagna involves preparing a savory meat sauce, a creamy cheese filling, layering them with noodles, and baking until bubbly. The process takes roughly 45-60 minutes to bake in an oven set to 375°F (190°C), followed by a necessary rest time to ensure clean slices.

    Ingredients
  • Meat Sauce
  • Cheese Filling
  • Noodles & Topping
    Steps
  1. Prepare the Meat Sauce
  2. Prepare the Noodles & Cheese
  3. Assemble the Lasagna
  4. Bake
  5. Rest and Serve
